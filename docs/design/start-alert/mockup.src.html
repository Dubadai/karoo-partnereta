PartnerETA: "Alex started riding"

While you're riding and your partner isn't yet, PartnerETA tells you once when they start: a Karoo in-ride alert (the Karoo draws it; we give it an icon, a title, a detail and colours). It waits until the ETA has settled, so the time in it is worth reading. Once per ride per person, never while you're not recording.

When it shows: they've ridden 5 minutes on their route and their ETA has stayed within 2 minutes for the last 2 minutes (at the latest after 10 minutes, with the ETA as it is then). If they were already riding when you started, no alert. Stays 15 seconds, or until you tap it.

Your pick: the finish line. The live link only has their route as a line, no place names. A: a street address, looked up once per ride from OpenStreetMap (sends that one point to OpenStreetMap's server). B: no lookup, where their finish is from yours.
