PartnerETA: "Alex is riding"

While you're riding and your partner isn't yet, PartnerETA tells you once when they start: the Karoo's own in-ride alert (the Karoo draws it; we give it an icon, a title, a detail and colours). It shows as soon as their live link says they're riding, so you know they've left. Their ETA is in the field as usual; it settles over the first minutes.

When it shows: you're recording, they weren't riding when you started, and they start. Once per ride per person. Stays 15 seconds, or until you dismiss it.

The finish (A, our pick): the street of their route's end, looked up once per ride from OpenStreetMap (sends that one point to OpenStreetMap's server). If the lookup takes longer than 10 seconds, the alert goes without it. The Karoo shows a capitals title of about 17 characters and two detail lines, so the text is short: "38.2 km route / to Hornsgatan 12" (round 3, after photos of the real alert).
